Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering and storage.

How do I place an order?

You can either add items to the cart on this website and click "Checkout via WhatsApp" or directly send a message on WhatsApp to 6304433996 with your required pickle and quantity. We will confirm your order and send bank/UPI details immediately.

What quantities are available? Can I order small trial packs or bulk?

As highlighted on our official poster, we provide quantities from 100 grams up to 5 kilograms! Whether you want a small 100g or 250g trial pack or large 5kg catering buckets for weddings, functions, and hostels, we have you covered.

What is the shelf life of Non-Veg and Veg Pickles?

- Non-Veg Pickles (Chicken, Mutton, Prawns): 2 to 3 months when stored in the refrigerator with a clean, dry spoon. Always ensure a thin layer of oil rests on top.
- Veg Pickles (Gongura, Avakaya, Pandumirchi, Tomato, Allam): 6 to 12 months in a cool, dry place.
- Karam Podulu: 4 to 6 months in an airtight jar.

How is the shipping handled? Is there doorstep delivery?

Yes! We provide safe doorstep delivery across India through trusted courier partners (DTDC, Professional Couriers, Speed Post). Items are packed in heavy-duty food-grade tamper-proof jars with multi-layer bubble wrap to guarantee zero leakage.
