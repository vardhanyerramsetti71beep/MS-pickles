# MS Pickles - Authentic Andhra Homemade Pickles & Spice Powders

> **"Homemade Taste • Authentic Andhra Flavour"**  
> **"నిజమైన రుచి... ఇంటి వంటల అభిమానం..."**  
> WhatsApp Helpline & Direct Orders: **6304433996**

A lively, responsive, high-converting e-commerce website built directly from the official **MS Pickles** poster.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Poster Integration

1. **Brand Identity & Atmosphere**:
   - Royal Andhra emerald green, deep spice gold, and fiery red chili color scheme.
   - Lively floating spice animations (red chilies, curry leaves, garlic, mustard seeds).
   - High-impact hero spotlight for the Star of the show: **Spicy Chicken Pickle (చికెన్ పచ్చడి)**.
   - Interactive **View Official Poster** modal with full zoom and authentic background story.

2. **All 11 Authentic Products from the Poster**:
   - **Non-Veg Pickles (మాంసాహారం)**:
     - చికెన్ పచ్చడి (Spicy Chicken Pickle)
     - మటన్ పచ్చడి (Royal Mutton Pickle)
     - రొయ్యల పచ్చడి (Coastal Prawns Pickle)
   - **Veg Pickles (శాకాహారం)**:
     - గోంగూర పచ్చడి (Authentic Gongura Pachadi)
     - పండుమిర్చి పచ్చడి (Pandu Mirchi Pachadi)
     - అల్లం పచ్చడి (Traditional Allam Pachadi)
     - టమాట పచ్చడి (Country Tomato Pachadi)
     - మామిడి పచ్చడి (Andhra Avakaya)
   - **Traditional Karam Podulu (కారప్పొడులు)**:
     - కరివేపాకు కారప్పొడి (Curry Leaf Spice Powder)
     - మునగాకు కారప్పొడి (Moringa / Drumstick Leaf Powder)
     - ధనియాల కారప్పొడి (Coriander Seed Powder)

3. **Flexible Weight Selector (100g to 5kg)**:
   - Direct support for all sizes mentioned on the poster: **100g, 250g, 500g, 1kg, 2kg, and 5kg**.
   - Real-time price calculation with discount incentives on larger packs.

4. **Frictionless Ordering Experience**:
   - **1-Click WhatsApp Ordering**: Generates formatted, ready-to-send WhatsApp messages to **+91 6304433996** with the customer's name, address, chosen sizes, and itemized bill.
   - **Interactive Slide-Out Cart**: Add items, adjust quantities, see live subtotal and free delivery progress.
   - **Printable Bill / Invoice Generator**: Clean printable receipt with customer details and order ID.
   - **Bilingual Support**: English & Telugu names with a 1-click toggle for easy reading.
   - **Filter & Search**: Filter by Non-Veg, Veg, or Podi, filter by Spice Level (Mild, Medium, Fiery Hot), or search in English or Telugu!

## 🚀 How to Run

### Option 1: Double-Click
Simply double-click `start_website.bat` in Windows. It will open `http://localhost:8080` in your default browser.

### Option 2: Terminal / PowerShell
```powershell
cd d:\PROJECTS\ms-pickles
python server.py
```
Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080) in your web browser.
